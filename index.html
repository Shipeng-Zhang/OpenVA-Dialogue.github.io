01 / OVERVIEW

Conversation is a
continuous signal.

Realtime Dialogue Generation treats interaction as a living stream instead of a sequence of isolated turns. Our model jointly predicts visual presence, speech, and listening behavior — allowing natural interruptions, backchannels, and long-form context to emerge.

The examples below are generated from one shared model interface. Each sample highlights a different capability.
